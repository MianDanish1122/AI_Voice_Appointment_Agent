Talk to the receptionist: ask about services, describe what you need, and book a meeting that lands on Google Calendar in real time.

Press “Start call” and allow the microphone.

Things to try

  • “What services do you offer, and how much does a website cost?”
  • “I’d like to book a meeting tomorrow at 3 PM.” — then change your mind: “Actually, make it Monday at 11.”
  • Ask for a Sunday or 10 PM — it will suggest open times.
  • When asked, spell your email (“john at gmail dot com”).
  • “Can I talk to a real person?”
